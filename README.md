# 🛡️ ShieldMesh Lite

**Accessible Cyber Defense for Everyone.**

## 👋 Welcome to My First Project!
Hello! I built ShieldMesh Lite because I noticed a massive problem: most cybersecurity tools are built for giant corporations. They are too expensive, too complex, and full of technical jargon. 

Meanwhile, everyday digital users, students, and small business owners are the ones who frequently lose money and data to online scams without ever knowing they are vulnerable. 

## 💡 What is ShieldMesh Lite?
ShieldMesh Lite is a simple, 1-page web tool. It acts as an automated safety scanner. 

You don't need to be a tech expert to use it. Simply enter a suspicious web link or your email address, and the tool will give you an instant safety audit report telling you if you are safe, or if you need to take action.

## 🎯 Who is this for?
*   **Small Business Owners** trying to keep customer data safe.
*   **Students** navigating the web.
*   **Everyday Digital Users** who want peace of mind without the jargon.

## 🛠️ How it was built (The MVP)
This is the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) built during a One-Week Challenge. It uses standard web languages (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript) to create a clean, easy-to-understand interface. Future versions will integrate with real-time APIs (like Have I Been Pwned) to provide live vulnerability data.
